Important reality check

KLM has its own flight school, the KLM Flight Academy (KFA). Its published programme is designed to train people from the beginning. However, admission is selective and the academy's current rules create some important hurdles for an applicant educated in Canada.

1. The direct KLM pathway

Apply to KLM Flight Academy. The academy describes a roughly two-year course and says successful graduates may enter KLM, subject to successfully completing training and the conditions in the employment agreement.

Primary option

2. The alternative pathway

Complete EASA professional pilot training at another approved European flight school, build airline experience, and later apply for a KLM vacancy if you meet the requirements at that time.

Backup option
Three things to investigate early: KFA currently lists Dutch or EU-member-state nationality, very good Dutch, and specific academic subjects. Its page says a foreign diploma must be assessed by Nuffic and additional Dutch-level mathematics and physics certificates are required. Confirm your personal eligibility with KFA before spending money on training.

What this plan assumes

  • You are starting without a pilot licence or logged flight hours.
  • You are currently in Canada and will need to plan for Canadian secondary-school credentials.
  • You are interested in KLM specifically, not just any airline.
  • All admissions, immigration, licensing and hiring rules must be checked again when you apply; they can change.

The complete roadmap

Follow these stages in order. Some can overlap, but do not pay for flight training before checking medical and eligibility requirements.

01

Understand the profession

Learn what airline pilots do: flight planning, weather decisions, aircraft systems, checklists, radio communication, teamwork, fatigue management and passenger safety. Start learning aviation terminology and basic aerodynamics.

02

Build your school foundation

Complete secondary school. Prioritize mathematics, physics, English and Dutch. KFA's published Dutch admission page specifies different subject combinations for HAVO and VWO, and asks applicants with foreign qualifications to obtain a Nuffic assessment and additional Dutch-level maths and physics certificates.

03

Check citizenship and right to study/work

KFA currently lists Dutch or EU-member-state nationality. KLM's direct pilot recruitment page also lists a valid passport from an EU member state (and certain associated European states). Canadian citizenship alone does not meet that published nationality requirement. Ask KFA and KLM recruitment what documents they accept and whether your circumstances qualify.

04

Reach the required Dutch and English level

KFA says applicants who are not fluent in spoken and written Dutch cannot participate and advises at least B2 if learning Dutch. KLM's direct pilot vacancy criteria require fluent Dutch and English; non-native Dutch speakers must pass a B2 Dutch exam before selection.

05

Check aviation medical eligibility

Before committing to expensive training, arrange an initial EASA Class 1 medical with an authorized aviation medical examiner. Ask about eyesight, colour vision, hearing, general health and any individual concerns. Only the authorized medical process can determine whether you qualify.

06

Apply to KLM Flight Academy

When you meet the published entry conditions, submit the required school records and certificates. KFA's selection currently includes a pre-selection, basic aptitude assessment, sensorimotor assessment, competency assessment and a final admissions interview. Its published selection period is approximately three to six months.

07

Complete professional flight training

Study aviation theory, fly single-engine aircraft, progress to multi-engine aircraft, complete instrument training and commercial flight training, then complete multi-crew cooperation and aircraft type-rating training as required by the programme.

08

Graduate with the required licences and ratings

KFA says successful graduates receive a Frozen ATPL(A), CPL(A), multi-engine and instrument qualifications, night qualification, radio-telephony and language endorsements, MCC certificate and a type rating. The exact package can change, so check the course details for your intake.

09

Complete airline induction and start flying

After successful training and satisfaction of the employment agreement's conditions, move into the relevant KLM cockpit position. Airline-specific training, checks and supervised operating experience apply. You continue recurrent simulator and proficiency checks throughout your career.

10

Build your airline career

Gain experience, maintain medical and licence validity, complete recurrent training and develop as a professional crew member. Career progression depends on performance, seniority, operational needs and vacancies.

Flight training explained

These are the main licences and qualifications you will hear about. The KLM Flight Academy route is integrated, so you may not need to obtain every item separately before entering.

Starting from zero

Ab-initio training

This means training from the beginning. You do not need previous flight hours to start an approved ab-initio course, provided you meet that school's entry requirements.

What happens at KLM Flight Academy?

KFA currently describes four phases over approximately two years:

  1. About 9 months: theoretical training at Eelde.
  2. About 6 months: single-engine flight training at Eelde.
  3. About 6 months: multi-engine flying, including the CPL phase.
  4. About 3 months: integrated MCC/type-rating training at Schiphol.
A Frozen ATPL is not the same as holding the full ATPL. Under EASA rules, the full aeroplane ATPL has a minimum age of 21 and requires the applicable training, examinations, experience and skill test.

Training phases and durations are KFA's published outline, not a guarantee of an individual's graduation date.

KLM and KLM Flight Academy requirements

There are two different sets of rules: entry to the flight academy, and direct recruitment by KLM as an already-qualified pilot. Do not confuse them.

KLM Flight Academy — published admission points

  • Age: at least 17 at the start of training (current published rule).
  • Nationality: Dutch or a nationality of an EU member state.
  • Language: very good spoken and written Dutch; KFA says at least B2 if you are learning Dutch. English is also required academically.
  • Education: acceptable secondary-school diploma and required subjects. HAVO: mathematics B, physics, English and Dutch, with an additional physics course in the specified circumstances. VWO: mathematics A or B, physics, English and Dutch.
  • Foreign education: KFA says a foreign diploma must be assessed by Nuffic. It additionally asks for Dutch HAVO-level certificates in physics and mathematics B, with an additional physics course in the stated circumstances.
  • Medical/physical: KFA publishes a height range of 158–203 cm and medical/vision conditions. Check its current medical FAQ and obtain professional advice; the formal medical examination decides medical fitness.
Important for a Canadian applicant: KFA's foreign-diploma process does not automatically mean every Canadian high-school diploma is accepted. Get a written assessment from KFA before choosing courses. The EU/Dutch nationality requirement is a separate issue from the education requirement.

KLM direct pilot recruitment — published requirements

KLM's current cockpit recruitment page for experienced pilots lists, among other things:

  • Valid passport from an EU member state, including Norway, Switzerland, Iceland and Liechtenstein.
  • Fluent Dutch and English; B2 Dutch exam for non-native Dutch speakers before selection.
  • EASA (Frozen) ATPL(A), valid multi-pilot aircraft type rating and Class 1 medical.
  • Multi-Crew Cooperation, initial CRM, English LPE6 and Advanced UPRT.
  • At least 500 hours on a multi-engine aircraft of 5,700 kg or more, and at least 150 hours in commercial aviation in the 12 months before applying, or the specified EU military-pilot alternative.

These are the published requirements for KLM's experienced-pilot vacancy route. They are not a checklist to complete before applying to KLM Flight Academy. KLM's cadet/academy route is different.

Selection

KFA currently describes a multi-stage selection process lasting around three to six months. KLM's experienced-pilot recruitment can include CV screening, online assessment, psychological assessment, simulator grading, interview, security checks and medical examination.

School and preparation

The best thing to do at the beginning is build the academic and language foundation. You do not need to rush into buying flight lessons.

Subjects to prioritize

Mathematics

Build confidence with algebra, equations, geometry, trigonometry, graphs and mental arithmetic. KFA's Dutch route specifically names mathematics B at HAVO level, or mathematics A/B at VWO level.

Physics

Learn forces, motion, pressure, energy, electricity, waves and basic mechanics. Physics is specifically required in KFA's published entry pathways.

English

Build strong listening, speaking, reading and technical vocabulary. Aviation communication depends on clear, precise English.

Dutch

Make Dutch a long-term priority if KLM Flight Academy remains the target. Work towards fluent speaking, reading and writing, then formal B2 proficiency.

Beginner aviation learning

  • Learn the four forces of flight: lift, weight, thrust and drag.
  • Understand how lift, angle of attack, stalls and weather work.
  • Learn the parts of an aircraft and the purpose of the flight controls.
  • Practice reading a basic map, compass directions and time calculations.
  • Use a flight simulator as a learning aid, not as a replacement for real instruction.
  • Watch real cockpit and aviation safety material, and learn standard phraseology gradually.
Do not self-diagnose medical eligibility. Glasses or contact lenses do not automatically rule someone out. The applicable EASA Class 1 medical standards and an authorized examiner determine eligibility.

Canadian education question to resolve

Ask KFA how a British Columbia Dogwood Diploma or other Canadian secondary-school credential is assessed, what Nuffic documentation is needed, and exactly how to satisfy its Dutch mathematics B and physics certificate requirements. Get the answer in writing and keep it in your records.

Costs and financing

Professional flight training is expensive. The exact cost depends heavily on the school and pathway, and prices can change each year.

KLM Flight Academy published financing information

KFA's current financing page says that for 2026 the total training cost is €163,500. It describes an arrangement where students receive an employment agreement with KLM subject to conditions, and an education allowance of €700 gross per month during training. After successful graduation and entering employment, the stated training cost can be paid at once or deducted from gross salary over ten years; the page lists €861 per month for 2026, subject to annual indexation.

Do not treat this as guaranteed admission or guaranteed employment. Read the current contract carefully with a parent/guardian and an independent adviser. Ask what happens if you withdraw, fail a stage, become medically unfit, or do not satisfy the conditions for employment.

Budget items to ask about

  • Tuition and financing charges, if any.
  • Accommodation and living costs in the Netherlands.
  • Travel between Canada and the Netherlands.
  • Medical examination and any follow-up assessments.
  • Language courses and required subject certificates.
  • Visa/residence or administrative costs, if applicable to your status.
  • Equipment, books, uniform and examination fees.

The published KFA financing figures above are a 2026 snapshot. Confirm all numbers and contract terms directly with KFA before relying on them.
